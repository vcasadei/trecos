Know what’s in every box.

Trecos is a home inventory for Android. Record what you own, which box, drawer or shelf it lives in, and what it is worth. It stays on your phone, with no account and no ads.

Download the APK Free. Android 9 and later. Google Play coming soon.

Features

{{shot house-list|The house list: Apartment, Beach house and Parents' house, with item counts and values}}

Every house, every box

Keep an apartment, a beach house and your parents’ attic apart. Inside each house, put boxes in shelves in rooms, as many levels deep as you need.

  • Each item shows its full path: Apartment › Office › Box A
  • Move, copy or duplicate items and boxes, also between houses
  • Deleted things wait 30 days in the trash
{{shot item|An item with quantity, unit price, total value, brand, model and serial number}}

The details insurance asks for

Quantity, unit price, brand, model, serial number, photos and your own fields. Values add up per box and per house, in your currency.

  • Two-level categories, with suggestions that work offline, and free-form tags
  • Photos are stored without their location data
{{shot search|Search results for “usb” across two houses, with the path to each item}}

Find anything in seconds

Type part of a name and Trecos searches every house, ignoring accents. Filter by house, category or tag, or search only inside one box.

  • Print QR labels, stick them on boxes and scan one to see what is inside
  • It also reads QR codes made by other programs
  • Backups Export everything to a file and restore it on any phone.
  • Google Drive sync Optional, to your own Drive, across your phones.
  • White and Dark A soft white theme and a pure black one.
  • English and Portuguese Brazilian Portuguese throughout.

Private by design

Your inventory says what you own and where it is. Trecos is built so that nobody else sees it, including us.

  • No account, no server There is no Trecos server. The developer never receives your data.
  • Works offline Everything lives on your phone and works without a connection.
  • No ads, no tracking No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising code. A check in every build keeps them out.
  • Your own Drive Sync is optional and goes to your Google Drive. Trecos can open only the files it created.
  • App lock Unlock with your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password.
  • Few permissions No camera or storage permission: photos come from the system camera and photo picker.

Database encryption is available as an experimental option. The source code is public, so anyone can check all of this. Read the privacy policy
