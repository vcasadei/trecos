Know what’s in every box.

Trecos is a home inventory for Android. Record what you own, which box, drawer or shelf it lives in, and what it is worth, across every house you look after.

Download the APK Free. Android 9 and later. Google Play coming soon.

Boxes inside boxes

Houses hold rooms, rooms hold shelves, shelves hold boxes, as deep as you need. Every item shows its full path: Apartment › Office › Box A.

Values for insurance

Quantity, unit price, brand, model, serial number and photos. Totals add up per box and per house, in your own currency.

QR labels

Print a label, stick it on the box, scan it later to see what is inside without opening it.

Your data stays yours

Works offline, with no account and no server. Optional sync goes to your own Google Drive. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Support Trecos

Trecos is free and has no ads. If it helps you keep track of your things, you can help keep it going.
