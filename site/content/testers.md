# Test Trecos before launch

Trecos is a free home inventory app for Android 9 and later: what you own,
where it is stored and what it is worth, with QR labels. It works offline, with
no account and no ads. Before it reaches the Play Store for everyone, it needs
testers. Thank you for helping.

## How to join

1. **Join the testers group** with the Google account you use on your Android
   phone: open <https://groups.google.com/g/trecos-testers> and click **Join
   group**.
2. **Become a tester:** open <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.trecos>,
   signed in with the same account, and tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install Trecos** from the Play Store:
   <https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.trecos>. It can take a few
   minutes after step 2 before the Play Store shows it.

Please **stay in the test for at least 14 days**. You don't need to use the app
every day, but leaving the test or the group before then resets Google's count.

## What to try

- Create your home, a few rooms and boxes, and put boxes inside boxes.
- Add items with a photo, a price and a category.
- Search for something, and use the filters.
- Make a QR label for a box, print or share it, and scan it back.
- Try the dark theme, and Portuguese or English in Settings.

## Tell us what you find

Anything that breaks, looks wrong or is confusing helps, even small things. A
screenshot helps most. Send it through the Play Store (Trecos page, "Send
feedback to the developer" in the testing section), to <hello@trecos.app>, or as
an issue at <https://github.com/vcasadei/trecos/issues>.

Your e-mail is only used by Google Play to give you access to the test; group
members can't see each other's addresses. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
