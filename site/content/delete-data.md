# Deleting your Trecos data

Trecos, the home inventory app by Vitor Casadei, has no accounts and no server.
The developer never receives your data, so there is nothing to ask us to delete:
everything is on your phone and, only if you turned on sync, in your own Google
Drive. You delete it yourself, at any time, with the steps below.

## On your phone

- **Everything at once:** uninstall Trecos, or open Android **Settings → Apps →
  Trecos → Storage → Clear storage**. This deletes all houses, items, photos,
  settings, your optional profile (name and e-mail) and the database encryption
  key kept on the phone.
- **One house:** in Trecos, open the house's menu and choose **Delete
  permanently**. Its items, trash and photos are removed for good.
- **Single items:** delete them in the app; they stay in that house's trash for
  30 days, or until you empty the trash.

## In your Google Drive (only if you used sync)

1. In Trecos, if **Encrypt data** is on, turn it off first (Settings). Then open
   **Settings → Sync** and choose **Disconnect**, on every phone that syncs.
2. In Google Drive, delete the **Trecos** folder in My Drive and empty Drive's
   trash. This removes the synced houses, items, photos, sync records (which
   carry your Google account's name and e-mail and each device's name) and
   older syncs.
3. Turning encryption off already removes its key from Drive. To be sure
   nothing is left, delete the hidden app data: in
   Google Drive on the web, open **Settings → Manage apps**, find **Trecos**,
   and choose **Options → Delete hidden app data**.

## What is kept

Nothing is kept by the developer. Tips and ratings are handled by Google Play
under Google's own policies. Files you exported yourself (backups, shared QR
labels) stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
