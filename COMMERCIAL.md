Trecos is free for any **noncommercial** purpose under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE): personal use, research,
education, hobby projects, and use by charities, public bodies and schools.

## When you need a commercial license

| Use | License needed |
|---|---|
| Using the app for your own home inventory | None |
| A charity, school or public body using it | None |
| Modifying the code for personal or noncommercial use | None |
| Selling the app, or a modified version of it | Commercial |
| Shipping Trecos code inside a paid or ad-supported product | Commercial |
| A business using a modified build internally for profit-making work | Commercial |

When in doubt, ask.

## How to ask

Open a GitHub issue titled **"Commercial license request"** with your company
name and a sentence on the intended use; the maintainer will reply and move
the conversation off GitHub. Keep confidential details out of the issue.

Commercial licenses are offered by Vitor Casadei, who holds the rights to
Trecos and to contributions under the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).
Third-party components included in Trecos keep their own licenses, which
commercial terms don't change.
