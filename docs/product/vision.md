# Vision

Trecos (Brazilian slang for "stuff") is a free, source-available, offline-first
home inventory app for Android 9 and later. It records **what you own**,
**where it is physically stored**, and **what it is worth**, across several
houses, with printable QR labels and optional sync to your own Google Drive.

It is built for home and maker inventories: cables, adapters, boards, tools and
spares spread over rooms, shelves and boxes. The original request is kept in
[original-draft.md](original-draft.md).

## Principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Offline first | Every feature except sync, QR scanning, tips and rating works with no network |
| Your data stays yours | No server, no account system, no analytics, no ads, ever |
| Light and fast | Smooth on a 2 GB RAM Android 9 phone; cold start of 1.5 s or less |
| Simple, sophisticated UI | Minimal screens, pastel colours, short animations, text that never breaks layouts |
| Two languages from day one | English and Portuguese (Brazil) |
| Source-available | [PolyForm Noncommercial](../../LICENSE); commercial use by licence ([COMMERCIAL.md](../../COMMERCIAL.md)) |

## Who it is for

- People who want to know where things are across a home, a second home or a
  parents' house.
- Makers and hobbyists with many small, similar parts.
- Anyone who needs an inventory with values, for moving house or insurance.

## Not in v1

OCR prefill, house sharing, attribute extraction, a grid view for large
screens, photo search and multiple users on one phone. They are ordered in the
[roadmap](roadmap.md).
