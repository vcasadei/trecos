# Backup

A backup is one `.zip` file you can keep anywhere and import on any phone. It
works without a Google account.

## Exporting

1. Settings > **Sync & backup** > **Backup**.
2. Under **Export**, untick any house you want to leave out (all are ticked).
3. Tap **Export** and choose where to save, for example Downloads or a USB
   drive. The file is named like `trecos-backup-2026-09-28.zip`.

The backup holds every item, container, category, tag, custom field and
full-size photo of the chosen houses, including what is in their trash.
Thumbnails are left out and rebuilt after an import.

The file is **not encrypted**. Keep it somewhere safe.

If saving fails, for example because the drive is full, Trecos says so and
removes the incomplete file.

## Importing

1. Settings > **Sync & backup** > **Backup** > **Import a backup**, and pick the file.
2. Trecos shows what the backup holds: each house with its containers, items
   and photos.
3. Choose:
   - **Add as new houses**: the backup's houses appear next to yours, as new
     houses. Nothing of yours changes.
   - **Replace everything**: after you confirm, everything on this phone is
     deleted and replaced by the backup. This can't be undone.

An import is all or nothing. If the file is damaged, or made by a newer version
of Trecos (update the app first), nothing on the phone changes.
