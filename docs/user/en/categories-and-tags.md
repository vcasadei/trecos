# Categories and tags

## Categories

Trecos comes with about 20 category groups and more than 100 subcategories,
from **Cables > USB-C** to **Tools > Safety & PPE**, in English and Portuguese.

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Give an item categories | In the item form, tap **Add category**, search, and tick one or more |
| Choose the main category | The first one is the main one; its icon shows in lists. In the picker, tap **Set as main** on another |
| Use a suggestion | While you type the name or description, chips suggest up to three categories. Tap one to add it. Nothing is added unless you tap |
| Create your own | In the picker, tap **New category**: a name, where it goes (under a group or as its own group) and an icon |
| Delete your own | In the picker, open its menu (⋮) and delete it. It's removed from every item; items keep their other categories |

Built-in categories can't be renamed or deleted. Your own categories belong to
the house you created them in, and are shown exactly as you typed them.

Suggestions work offline. They know common words in English and Portuguese
("cabo usb-c", "power bank", "luvas de látex") and learn your own words: after
you tag a few items named "rpi" as **SBCs & dev boards**, typing "rpi" suggests it.

## Tags

Tags are your own system: "borrowed", "broken", "for sale"…

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Add a tag | In the item form, tap **More fields**, type the tag and tap **+**. Existing tags are offered as you type |
| Rename or delete a tag | Home > house menu (⋮) > **Tags**. The change applies to every item |

"Borrowed", "borrowed" and "BORROWED" are the same tag. Tags belong to the house.

## A new house

When you add a house you can copy another house's categories and tags into
it. Items are never copied.
