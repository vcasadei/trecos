# Moving, copying and deleting

## Move, copy, duplicate

Open an item or container, tap ⋮, and choose:

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Move** | Pick a destination: browse, search, or switch to another house first. A container moves with everything inside it. It can't go inside itself |
| **Copy** | Same picker. The copy is a new thing with **no QR code**, because a label identifies one physical object. A copied container includes copies of everything inside |
| **Duplicate** | A copy in the same place, named "… (copy)", opened for editing |

Moving to another house brings the item's own categories and tags along; any
that don't exist there are created. If a QR code is already used in that
house, you choose to change the code, remove it, or cancel.

### Several at once

Long-press a row to start selecting, tap more rows, then use the buttons at
the top: **Select all**, **Move**, **Copy** or **Delete**. Back clears the
selection without changing anything.

## Delete and the trash

Every delete asks first. Right after deleting, **Undo** brings it back.

Deleted things go to the house's **trash** (house menu ⋮ > **Trash**) for 30
days, then they are removed for good. From the trash you can **restore**
something to where it was (you choose a new place if that's gone) or delete it
permanently. Things in the trash don't show in lists or values.

### Deleting a box with things inside

You can send everything to the trash, or tap **Choose what to keep**: tick
things, tap **Move selected to…** (the level above, a new container there, or
any container), repeat as needed, then **Finish** to trash the box with
whatever is left. Leaving without Finish deletes nothing.

### Deleting a house

House menu ⋮ > **Delete**, then type the house's name. This is **permanent**:
the house, its contents and its trash are removed. Export a backup first. The
last house can't be deleted.
