# 0017. Monetization without ads

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Change:** `trecos-v1` (design D18; spec help-and-support)

## Context

The app is free forever and must never show ads.

## Decision

An in-app **tip jar** (three repeatable Play Billing tips in Settings), **external donations** (GitHub Sponsors, Buy Me a Coffee, Pix) only on GitHub and trecos.app, not linked in the app, and a **commercial licence** contact. No supporter extras, no paid listing.

## Consequences

No external payment links in the app, to stay within Play policy.
